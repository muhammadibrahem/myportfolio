About Me

The Story Behind the Code

A passionate CS student from Lahore, building real projects and learning every single day.

Lahore, Pakistan CS — 4th Semester Open to Remote muhammadibrahem

Hi, I'm Muhammad Ibrahim — a frontend developer who believes the best way to learn is to build.

I'm currently in my 4th semester of Computer Science, and I've spent that time doing something concrete: building over 13 real projects. From a weather app to a drawing canvas, from a movie finder to a full restaurant UI — every project has been a lesson in disguise.

My core stack is the fundamental trio: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I'm a firm believer that you need to deeply understand the foundations before layering on frameworks. That's why I spend time mastering the basics — DOM manipulation, the Fetch API, Canvas, localStorage — before reaching for libraries.

I'm currently expanding into ReactJS and learning how to structure larger applications. My goal is to become a well-rounded frontend developer who can take a design from concept to a fast, accessible, responsive reality.

Beyond code, I'm curious by nature. I enjoy exploring UI design, reading about web performance, and occasionally breaking things just to understand how they work. If you have a project or opportunity, I'd love to connect.
